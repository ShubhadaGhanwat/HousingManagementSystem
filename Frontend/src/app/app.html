About Our Society

More Than a Home,
A Community.

Simple Park Society, Sasane Nagar, is a community built on togetherness, comfort, and shared responsibility. We believe that a great home is more than four walls — it's a place where neighbours connect and families thrive.

Our goal is to make society living simpler through better communication, organised management, and active participation from every resident.
